# Urban Noise Pollution in Dhaka

Statistical analysis of noise levels across 7 sites in Dhaka, based on a primary field survey.

**Tools:**   SPSS, Excel  

**Period:** Aug 2026 – Present

## Key Findings

- Average noise level was **82.0 dB(A)**, about 12 dB above the 70 dB(A) commercial threshold (p < .001).
- **All 7 sites** exceeded the threshold.
- **Location** affected noise levels more than **time of day** (two-way ANOVA).
- A 5-minute motorcycle test averaged **103.3 dB(A)**.
- **90%** of respondents reported noise-related disturbance, headaches, or sleep problems.

| Panthapath | 79.5 (1.29) |
| **All locations (n = 63)** | **82.0 (2.95)** |

Measurements were taken with a smartphone app (screening-level, not calibrated) during 3-7 May 2026, three readings per site in each of three periods (morning, afternoon, evening).

## Methods

- Hypothesis testing against the 70 dB(A) commercial threshold
- Two-way ANOVA (location × time of day)
- Descriptive analysis and data organization in Excel and SPSS
